Harvey Luna is the [Operations Manager](https://www.smu.edu/simmons/about-us/directory/center-on-research-evaluation/luna) at [The Addy Foundation Center on Research and Evaluation (CORE) at Southern Methodist University (SMU)](https://www.smu.edu/simmons/research/center-on-research-evaluation). He has served at SMU for 10 years. His responsibilities include building and maintaining financial reporting systems, and leading data infrastructure work for a variety of research projects. Harvey primarily works in the education field with non-profits, foundations, and school districts.

Harvey began his journey in the data world as a data collector in the remote islands of Galapagos, working with CORE and the [Galapagos Conservancy](https://www.galapagos.org/) to evalute a new teacher professional development program on the islands. Since then, he has explored the full pipeline from instrument and evaluation deign, collecting data on the ground, processing data, analysing and reporting, and disseminating information to clients and at professional conferences. 

Harvey is currently a [Masters student in Data Science](https://datascience.smu.edu/) at Southern Methodist University, further pusuing his path in data science, analytics, and visualization. He obtained his Bachelor of Arts in Political Science and Bachelor of Science in Economics with Financial Applications from Southern Methodist University in 2014. Harvey currently holds CITI certification for Human Subjects Research (HSR) and Social & Behavioral Research.
